Water is a chemical compound with the formula H2O: two hydrogen atoms covalently bonded to a single
oxygen atom. At standard atmospheric pressure, water boils at 100 degrees Celsius (212 degrees Fahrenheit)
and freezes at 0 degrees Celsius (32 degrees Fahrenheit). It is the only common substance found naturally
on Earth's surface in all three physical states of matter: solid (ice), liquid, and gas (water vapor).

Water is essential to all known forms of life, even though it provides no calories or organic nutrients.
About 71 percent of Earth's surface is covered by water, mostly in oceans and other large water bodies,
with 1.6 percent of water below ground in aquifers and 0.001 percent in the air as vapor, clouds, and
precipitation.
